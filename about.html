About Software License Direct

Software License Direct is an authorized Microsoft partner. We sell genuine Microsoft Office and Microsoft 365 licenses and help you get them installed.

Who we are

Software License Direct is a software licensing company and an authorized Microsoft partner serving customers in the United States. We help individuals choose the right Microsoft license, buy or renew it, and get it installed — with real people available to answer your questions.

What we sell

  • Microsoft 365 Personal subscriptions, including renewals
  • Office Home 2024 (one-time purchase)
  • Free installation help with every license we sell

How we work

  • You tell us which product you want through our form, chat or phone.
  • A specialist confirms the right license and the price before you buy. You are never under any obligation to purchase.
  • After purchase, you receive your license and we help you install it. We only connect to your computer remotely with your explicit permission, and you can end the session at any time.

Looking for help with a product you already own?

We focus on selling licenses. Microsoft provides support for its own products at support.microsoft.com.
